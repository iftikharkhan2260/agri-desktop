Download and place these 5 files in this folder before running/building:
- NotoSans-Regular.ttf
- NotoSans-Medium.ttf
- NotoSans-Bold.ttf
- NotoNastaliqUrdu-Regular.ttf
- NotoNastaliqUrdu-Bold.ttf

Free download:
https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Sans
https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Nastaliq+Urdu

These are referenced via @font-face in index.html so printed
receipts/PDFs (item 2) render correctly in both English and Urdu.
